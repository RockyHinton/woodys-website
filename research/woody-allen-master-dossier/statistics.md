# How these numbers were produced

**These are derived statistics, not cited ones.** With two exceptions, no source states
these figures; they are **counted from this dossier's own film records** in
[`films.md`](films.md), using the rules stated per entry. That makes them checkable — every
total can be re-derived from the enumerated lists — and it makes them dependent on the
accuracy of those records.

**The two external corroborations:**

- The **50-feature** total agrees with contemporaneous trade reporting describing
  *Coup de Chance* as Allen's 50th feature [S033], and the Madrid film as his 51st [S039].
- The **Academy / BAFTA / Golden Globe** totals are taken from a compiled awards register
  [S021], not derived, and one of them is internally inconsistent — see §8.

**Standing caveat.** The credits-provenance limitation in [`README.md`](README.md) §3
applies. Counts of *credits* (cinematographers, editors, designers) are only as good as the
underlying credit attributions, and the production design and costume records are weakly
sourced. Those counts are flagged accordingly.
