## Theatre

First Broadway credit **1960** (*From A to Z*, sketch material); first play as author
**1966** (*Don't Drink the Water*); *Play It Again, Sam* **1969-02-12** — the production in
which he first worked with **Diane Keaton** and **Tony Roberts**. Full record in
[`beyond-film.md`](beyond-film.md) §*Theatre*.

## Filmmaking career — shape

- **50 theatrical features** directed, 1966–2023; a 51st in production in 2026. Counting
  methodology in [`statistics.md`](statistics.md).
- Sustained an almost annual release rhythm from 1977 to 2017. **2018 was the first
  calendar year since 1981 with no new Allen film** [S020].
- **Production base shifts:** New York (1966–2004) → London (2005–2010) → mixed
  Europe/New York (2011–2019) → Spain and France (2020–2023) → Madrid (2026).
- **Four long-term financing arrangements:** United Artists (1971–1980); Orion
  (1982–1991); a succession of independents and studios (1992–2000, incl. TriStar,
  Sweetland/Miramax, DreamWorks); Gravier Productions from 2001, with varying
  co-financiers.

## Writing (books)

Five humour collections (1971, 1975, 1980, 2007, 2022); one autobiography (2020); one
novel (2025); one story collection announced for 2026. Full record in
[`beyond-film.md`](beyond-film.md) §*Books & Writing*.

## Jazz

Clarinet, in the New Orleans traditional idiom. A Monday-night residency in New York for
roughly fifty years — Michael's Pub from the 1970s, the Café Carlyle from 1997 — ending
with the city's closure in **2020-03**. First European tour **1996**, documented in
*Wild Man Blues*. Band led by **Eddy Davis** from 1985 until his death in **2020-04**.
Full record in [`beyond-film.md`](beyond-film.md) §*Music & Jazz*.

## Scope note

The allegations made against Allen in 1992 and renewed publicly from 2014, and his
response to them, are outside this dossier's research scope. They appear in the timeline
**only** where they explain a dated distribution or publication event — the Amazon
dispute over *A Rainy Day in New York* (2019) and the Hachette/Arcade publication of
*Apropos of Nothing* (2020). See [`README.md`](README.md) §2.

source · **LOW** = flagged `[CHECK]` or `[CONFLICT]` in the timeline, do not publish
without verification.

**No event has been manufactured to fill a date.** There are large gaps in the calendar;
the website should handle days with no entry gracefully.

## Dates with unusual density

Useful for editorial planning:

- **04-20** — three events: *Don't Drink the Water* closes on Broadway (1968), *Annie Hall*
  released (1977), *To Rome with Love* released in Italy (2012).
- **05-11** — two Cannes opening films: *Midnight in Paris* (2011) and *Café Society*
  (2016).
- **08-18** — two US releases: *Take the Money and Run* (1969) and
  *Manhattan Murder Mystery* (1993).
- **09-04** — two Venice premieres: *Bullets Over Broadway* (1994) and *Coup de Chance*
  (2023).
- **10-14** — three events: *Another Woman* released (1988), *Bullets Over Broadway*
  released (1994), *Wonder Wheel* closes the New York Film Festival (2017).
- **11-30** — the birthday; **12-01** — *Wonder Wheel*'s release the day after an 82nd
  birthday. Treat the two together.

## January–February and June–July are the thinnest months

Not a data error — Allen's releases cluster around the spring (Cannes), late summer
(Venice) and autumn. An On This Day feature should be designed to say "no event recorded"
rather than to pad.
